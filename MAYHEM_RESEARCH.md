# The Mayhem Ball — visual research

The portfolio follows the show's four-act structure, finale and encore. The performance is treated as art direction and stage grammar; portfolio content remains the subject.

## Prologue

- Narrative role: house lights down; reveal the broken Opera House before the story begins.
- Dominant colours: near-black, aged stone, restrained blood red.
- Props and architecture: proscenium, balconies, columns, angel, ornament and velvet.
- Lighting: narrow overhead spotlight, dim red edge light, no full-stage wash.
- Useful photos: owner-supplied `opera-house-day.png` provides the cleanest separable architecture.
- Portfolio content: identity, discipline and primary navigation.

## ACT I — Of Velvet and Vice

- Songs: Bloody Mary, Abracadabra, Judas, Aura, Scheiße, Garden of Eden, Poker Face and the ballroom battle.
- Dominant colours: blood red, black, deep crimson; poisonous green arrives late.
- Props/costumes: giant red gown/cage, velvet curtains, quill silhouettes, opera balconies and chessboard language.
- Lighting: frontal crimson, hard black negative space, local acid-green contamination.
- Useful photos: owner `red-cage.png`, `red-opera-cage-wide.png`, `red-curtain-stage.png`, `red-opera-portal.png`; Commons Abracadabra and Scheiße angles.
- Portfolio content: current focus and Anonix project introduction.

## ACT II — And She Fell Into a Gothic Dream

- Songs: Perfect Celebrity, Disease, Paparazzi, LoveGame, Alejandro and The Beast.
- Dominant colours: bone white, sand grey, steel, cold blue, black and a small red seam.
- Props/costumes: grave/sandbox, skeletons, chrome crutches, immense white Paparazzi train, balcony and gothic architecture.
- Lighting: cold top light and steel edge light; occasional red runway geometry.
- Useful photos: Commons Disease grave and Beast runway; owner architecture can be regraded to bone/steel.
- Portfolio content: development disciplines in an asymmetric editorial layout.

## ACT III — The Beautiful Nightmare That Knows Her Name

- Songs: Killah, Zombieboy, LoveDrug, Applause and Just Dance.
- Dominant colours: violet, purple, acid green, black and cold stone.
- Props/costumes: giant skull, black staircase, roses and the Opera House in toxic light.
- Lighting: violet base, hard green rim, deep black sockets and foreground haze.
- Useful photos: owner `green-skull-stage.png` and `blue-skull.png` give wide and close skull angles; `purple-stage.png` supplies a distinct back plane; Commons Killah supplies the rose foreground.
- Portfolio content: technology stack, with primary stack dominant and secondary capabilities as technical annotations.

## ACT IV — Every Chessboard Has Two Queens

- Songs: Shadow of a Man, Kill for Love, Summerboy, Born This Way, Million Reasons, Shallow, Die With a Smile and Vanish Into You.
- Dominant colours: black, warm ivory, dark red and restrained gold.
- Props/costumes: light/dark Gaga, cloak, runway, gondola, lantern and chessboard motifs.
- Lighting: split light, ivory spotlight and warm lantern pool.
- Useful photos: owner `blue-boat-lantern.png`; Commons Shallow provides a second, wider gondola angle.
- Portfolio content: creative practice as a deliberate counterpart to code — Adobe, Photoshop, After Effects, motion, compositing and asset preparation.

## Mistress of Mayhem

- Dramatic role: the red-clad antagonist, double and ghost of an earlier self. She is a recurring pressure on the story, not the portfolio's subject.
- Visual progression on this site: barely visible in the Prologue; dominant inside the Act I cage/gown; reduced to one red trace in cold Act II; hidden beside the Act III skull; central to the Act IV dark/light confrontation; fragmented by print-noise masking in the Finale; entirely absent from Encore.
- Costumes and palette: opening crimson cage gown, red structured/bustled silhouettes and dark cloaked variants. Red remains the continuity signal even when the act palette turns bone, steel or violet.
- Poker Face: the stage becomes a life-sized chessboard for the confrontation between opposing selves. The diagonal board and interrupted figures are more useful than a symmetrical portrait.
- Million Reasons / Shallow: the relationship softens into an address between the two figures; the gondola, lantern and separated bodies provide the clearest Act IV spatial grammar.
- Ethereal relationship: the red/dark figure and the ivory/light figure cross the composition instead of occupying two sealed halves. The split supports the CODE / VISUAL argument without turning Gaga into page content.
- Finale resolution: white light and the angelic ensemble dominate while the red silhouette loses opacity through CSS masks and poster grain. This is a graphic transition, not a literal death animation.
- Mobile rule: one Mistress fragment at most per viewport, with architecture or prop still carrying the scene. Act I and Act IV are the only moments where she may read immediately.

## Finale — Eternal Aria of the Monster Heart

- Song: Bad Romance.
- Dominant colours: ivory, silver, black and tiny crimson details.
- Props/costumes: white angelic figure, wing-like gloves and bright Opera House opening.
- Lighting: sudden high-key stage spotlight against a still-dark theatre.
- Useful photos: Commons Bad Romance; owner angel/statue can echo the white silhouette without copying the tour logo.
- Portfolio content: selected work presented with less noise and a clear route into the Anonix case study.

## Encore

- Song: How Bad Do U Want Me; later dates also used G.U.Y. on tape.
- Narrative role: credits appear to end the show, then the performer returns from backstage in a simpler human register.
- Dominant colours: black, off-white, a single red indicator.
- Props: backstage texture and credits typography rather than spectacle.
- Portfolio content: direct contact and links.
